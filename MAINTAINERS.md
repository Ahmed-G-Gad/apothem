# Maintainers

**Purpose:** the GOVERNANCE roster — the people responsible for Apothem, who
review pull requests, cut releases, and participate in security response. This
is one of three related rosters, each with a distinct purpose:

- **[AUTHORS](AUTHORS)** — the copyright record (copyright holders, for
  licensing and attribution).
- **[CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md)** — everyone who has contributed,
  regenerated from git history.
- **MAINTAINERS.md** (this file) — the governance owners listed below.

For the machine-readable review-ownership partition, see
[CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS).
For how maintainers are added and removed, see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Becoming a maintainer

Apothem welcomes new maintainers. The path from contributor to maintainer
is described in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) under **Maintainer lifecycle**.
In short: sustained, high-quality contribution over time, demonstrated
judgement in code review and issue triage, and alignment with the project's
direction lead to an invitation from the project lead.

## Maintainer responsibilities

- **Pull-request review** — review assigned PRs within a reasonable window;
  uphold the quality bar described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Release cadence** — cut releases following the project's semantic-version
  scheme; keep [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) current.
- **Security response** — participate in coordinated disclosure per
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); honour the stated response and disclosure
  timelines.
- **Stewardship** — keep the contributor experience welcoming and the
  project's conventions consistent.
