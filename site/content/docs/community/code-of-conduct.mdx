Apothem is committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for
all contributors and users.

## Our standards

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accept constructive criticism.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Show empathy towards other community members.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or actions.
- Trolling, insulting, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Reporting

Report violations to [me@ahmedgad.com](mailto:me@ahmedgad.com).

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will review reports and take appropriate action,
including temporary or permanent bans for serious violations.

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
