This section documents the project's internal quality governance: the mandate
registries that define what every emitted artifact must satisfy, the gate that
checks artifacts before they ship, the inquiry categories that separate what
the tooling may decide from what it must ask, and the policies that govern
releases, badges, and the project's positioning.

These pages are normative for maintainers and for the automated checks in the
repository. Day-to-day usage guides live under [Guides](/docs/usage), and the
schemas and conventions they cite live under [Reference](/docs/reference).
