### Phase 1 — Ingest the Gap & Frame the Aims

Read `{suite}/_inputs/synthesis.md` and `{suite}/_inputs/theory.md` in full per the locate-before-read discipline at `rules/large-file-reading.md`. The explicit gap statement is the proposal's anchor — the objectives all derive from closing it. Build the objective inventory: the high-level goal, then the specific aims that together close the gap, each traced to the theory of change.

**A supplied funder call governs the proposal's shape.** When `--call` supplies the call text, read it in full: the funder's section structure, limits, applicant eligibility, review criteria, required attachments, and tool-use rules come from that text, never from recall, and no opportunity lookup runs. Externalise the **call-requirement matrix** to `{suite}/_inputs/call-requirements.md` (a free-form scratch file per `rules/context-management-scratch.md` §1), one row per requirement: its locator in the call text; its type (section, limit, eligibility, attachment, review criterion, policy); the proposal section that answers it; the draft location in the operator's draft (path and anchor, or `none`); and its status (`met`, `partly-met`, `gap`, `operator-owned`) with the gap action. A review criterion is a row, so each aim traces to the criterion it answers; no criterion gets a weight, and no claim about funding odds is made. When the operator supplies a draft, a criterion row also carries the draft's evidence for the criterion (path and anchor), the weakness that remains, and the proposed revision.

- **Identity records are placeholders.** Team, biographical statements, facilities, letters of support, and current-and-pending support belong to the operator (R6): each is an `operator-owned` row, never drafted.
- **Tool-use policy gate.** The call's policy on automated-tool use resolves first, to `permitted`; `permitted with disclosure` (the tool-and-model statement is drafted per `rules/authoritative-referencing-assurance.md` §5 and placed where the call asks); or `restricted`. The matrix restates the call and drafts no submission text, so it is written in every outcome. Plan text the operator MAY carry into a submission is written only when the policy is not `restricted`; under `restricted` the command stops after the matrix and returns the decision to the operator. A call silent on the policy is an operator inquiry, never an assumption.
